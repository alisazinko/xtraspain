About XtraSpain

Built by expats.
For expats.

We've been through the bureaucracy, the lost paperwork, and the appointments in Spanish. We built XtraSpain so you don't have to figure it out alone.

Our Story

From confusion
to community.

XtraSpain started as a personal project — notes, contacts, and hard-won lessons from the first years of living in Spain as a foreigner. What began as a shared Google Doc became a full service when we realised hundreds of others were going through the exact same struggle.

Today, we help English speakers from the UK, US, Ireland, Australia, and beyond navigate the process of making Spain their home — whether that's a digital nomad visa, a non-lucrative visa, or simply buying property in Valencia.

🌞

What we stand for

01

Radical Honesty

Spain is wonderful — and complicated. We tell you exactly what to expect, including the parts that are slow, frustrating, or expensive. No sugarcoating.

02

No Conflicts of Interest

We only refer collaborators we genuinely trust and have worked with personally. When we recommend a lawyer or broker, it's because they've delivered for our clients.

03

Always in English

Every interaction — calls, emails, documents — is in English. You'll never need to rely on Google Translate to understand the advice we give you.

04

Practical Over Perfect

We focus on what actually works for real people moving their lives to Spain — not theoretical best-case scenarios. Action beats analysis paralysis.
