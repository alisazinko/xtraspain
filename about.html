I moved to Spain from Russia in 2001, navigating the long road from my first residency permit to full Spanish citizenship. That process taught me everything the official guides leave out - the real timelines, the right contacts, and how to work with a system that does not always work with you.

Spain is a "country of countries." I know it firsthand.

Every region in Spain operates differently. The bureaucracy in Catalonia is not the bureaucracy in Andalusia. The pace of Malaga is not the pace of Madrid. Having lived in Barcelona, Zaragoza, Marbella, the Canary Islands, and Malaga, I understand the regional differences that catch most expats off guard.

When you work with XtraSpain, you're not getting generic advice pulled from a forum. You're getting guidance shaped by two decades of real life in multiple Spanish regions, across multiple visa categories, and through every layer of the immigration system.

Common Questions

About working with XtraSpain

Is XtraSpain a law firm? +

No. XtraSpain is an immigration consulting and relocation service. Alisa is a Licensed Immigration Professional, not a lawyer. For legal matters requiring an abogado (such as property purchases or court proceedings), we refer you to trusted partner lawyers in our collaborator network.

Do you only work with people from the UK? +

No. We work with English speakers from all countries - UK, USA, Canada, Australia, Ireland, South Africa, and beyond. The visa process differs depending on your nationality and whether you are an EU/EEA citizen, and we tailor our guidance accordingly.

Which regions of Spain do you cover? +

All of Spain. Alisa has personally lived in Barcelona, Zaragoza, Marbella, the Canary Islands, and Malaga, and has clients across the country. Our collaborator network covers Madrid, Valencia, Seville, Alicante, Bilbao, and the Costa del Sol.

Can you help families with children who have special educational needs? +

Yes, and this is an area where XtraSpain has unique expertise. Alisa personally navigated Spain's Special Education system for a child with autism, and now provides specialist support to families in the same situation. This includes identifying the right schools, understanding your rights under Spanish education law, and connecting you with the right professionals.

Ready to find your place in Spain?

Describe your situation and Alisa will come back to you with initial thoughts. No commitment - and any paid consultation fee is deducted if you go on to hire us.
