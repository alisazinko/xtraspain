01

Concierge Relocation Support

Moving to a new country brings up questions that Google simply cannot answer with certainty. This service is designed for those who need a direct line to a relocation expert - whether you are mid-application and hit a wall, or you have just landed and need to navigate the banking and healthcare systems.

What we solve

01

Expert Strategy Session

A dedicated call to analyse your specific situation - income, family structure, and goals - to determine your best legal path. No generic advice, just a clear strategy tailored to current regulations.

02

Local Infrastructure Setup

From choosing the right private health insurance that meets visa requirements, to opening a Spanish bank account without a residency card - we provide the introductions and paperwork support to get it done fast.

03

Document "Panic" Support

Received a Requerimiento (request for further evidence) from the government? Have 10 days to solve a problem you don't understand? We act as your emergency team to review, fix, and file the response.

04

Lifestyle & Soft-Landing Logistics

Guidance on digital signatures (Certificado Digital), local tax obligations, and finding English-speaking professionals - doctors, realtors, and accountants - in your specific city.
