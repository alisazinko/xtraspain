NLV

Non-Lucrative Visa Requirements 2026

For retirees and those with passive income who will not work in Spain.

The Non-Lucrative Visa (NLV) allows non-EU nationals to live in Spain without working. It is designed for retirees, people living on savings, pensions, dividends, or rental income from outside Spain. It does not permit any employment — including remote work. The initial permit is for 1 year, renewable to 2, then 5. The minimum income threshold is currently under review — verify the current figure with your consulate before applying.

Income threshold

€2,400/month Verified
400% of Spain's IPREM (€600/month in 2026)

With family members

Add 100% of IPREM per additional family member (€600/month per additional family member) Verified

Eligibility

  • No work of any kind permitted, including remote work for foreign employers
  • Must have sufficient passive income or savings
  • Income sources: pension, dividends, rental income, savings
