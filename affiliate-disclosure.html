What is an affiliate link?

An affiliate link is a tracked link to a product or service. If you click one and make a purchase or sign up, XtraSpain may earn a small commission from the company: at no extra cost to you.

Which links are affiliate links?

All affiliate links are marked with rel="sponsored nofollow" in the HTML and are accompanied by a note near the link stating that XtraSpain may earn a commission. We currently have affiliate relationships with:

  • Feather Insurance: health insurance for expats

Our editorial independence

We only recommend products we have vetted and believe are genuinely useful for people moving to Spain. Affiliate relationships do not influence which products we recommend or what we write about them. If we stop recommending something, we remove the affiliate link.
