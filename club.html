FAQ

About the Club

How is this different from the blog or free guides?

The blog and guides are general information. The Club lets you ask about your specific situation: your income type, your consulate, your timeline. General information tells you the rules. The Club helps you apply them to your case.

Is this legal advice?

No. The Club provides general immigration information and guidance. It does not create a lawyer-client relationship. For matters that require formal legal representation, use the complex case referral service.

How do I cancel?

You can cancel any time from your Lemon Squeezy account. No cancellation fee. Access continues until the end of the billing period you have already paid for.

What if I need a faster response?

The standard response time is 48 to 72 business hours. If you have an urgent matter with a government deadline, use the Done for You service instead.

Can I join the Club without buying a kit first?

Yes. The Club is open to anyone at any stage. That said, members who have the relevant Visa File Kit tend to ask more specific questions and get more targeted answers.

The questions don't stop after you buy the kit.

The Club is where you bring the ones that come up as you work through the process.
