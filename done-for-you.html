Sworn Translation

Official sworn (jurada) Spanish translation of your visa documents by a certified translator. Required for your criminal record, medical certificate, income documents, and more.

Background Check Coordination

We manage the apostille and coordination of your criminal record certificate from the FBI (US), RCMP (Canada), or ACRO (UK). The step that trips up most applicants.

  • Guidance on the correct form and submission method
  • Apostille coordination (Secretary of State / FCDO / Global Affairs)
  • Tracking and follow-up on your behalf
