01

Spanish VAT Registration & Compliance

If your company sells goods or services in Spain — or stores stock here — you are likely required to register for Spanish VAT. We manage the entire process and keep you compliant on an ongoing basis.

  • Modelo 036 registration with the AEAT for foreign and non-EU companies
  • Quarterly VAT return filings (Modelo 303) on your behalf
  • Annual VAT summary filing (Modelo 390)
  • Assessment of your VAT obligations in Spain before registration
  • Coordination with your accountant or CFO in your home country
  • All AEAT communications handled in Spanish — you receive clear summaries in English

Particularly relevant for UK companies that lost EU VAT simplifications post-Brexit, e-commerce businesses selling to Spanish consumers, and any company with a taxable presence (fixed establishment or warehouse) in Spain.

02

Director NIE Procurement

Foreign nationals acting as directors, signatories, or legal representatives of a company in Spain need a NIE. We guide you through the process — whether you are applying from abroad or already in Spain.

  • Assessment of whether a NIE is required for your role
  • Application preparation and document review (EX-15, supporting evidence)
  • Guidance on applying at the Spanish consulate in your home country
  • Guidance on in-person NIE appointment (cita previa) if you are in Spain
  • Proxy / gestor route coordination where applicable
  • Step-by-step support through every stage until the NIE certificate is issued
03

NIF Registration for Non-EU Companies

Any non-EU legal entity operating in Spain — signing contracts, opening accounts, or complying with Spanish tax law — needs a NIF. The process involves coordination between your home country and the AEAT. We handle it end to end.

  • AEAT application preparation and submission
  • Document checklist tailored to your company's country of registration
  • Apostille guidance for company documents (certificates of incorporation, directors, etc.)
  • Certified Spanish translation coordination for required documents
  • Liaison with the AEAT throughout the review process
  • Follow-up until the NIF is confirmed and active

The NIF is required before your company can be registered for VAT, enter into Spanish contracts, or open a Spanish bank account. Getting the document package right the first time saves weeks of back-and-forth.

04

Company Formation & Local Setup

Setting up a legal entity in Spain — whether a branch, subsidiary, or SL — involves multiple registrations across different authorities. We coordinate the process and make sure nothing falls through the gap.

  • Guidance on the right legal structure for your situation (branch vs subsidiary vs SL)
  • NIE and CIF procurement for directors and the entity itself
  • Notary and Commercial Registry coordination via our legal network
  • Social Security registration support for employees based in Spain
  • Local bank account opening guidance
  • Overview of local regulatory requirements for your industry
  • Introductions to vetted local lawyers, accountants, and advisors
05

Ongoing Operations Support

Once you are set up in Spain, day-to-day interactions with Spanish authorities, suppliers, and offices can still be time-consuming and opaque. We act as your local point of contact — so you do not need to.

  • Local vendor management and supplier communication in Spanish
  • Coordination with Spanish authorities on your behalf (AEAT, Seguridad Social, Ayuntamiento)
  • Process streamlining — identifying what you are required to do and what you can skip
  • Ad-hoc document handling, filing, and follow-up
  • Escalation point when something is blocked, delayed, or unclear
  • Regular English-language reporting on the status of your Spanish obligations

Designed for companies that have completed their initial setup and need reliable, ongoing local support without the cost of a full-time local employee.

06

Translation & Localization

Legal and administrative documents require precision. We provide professional translation and localization across three languages — with context, not just words.

  • Legal and administrative document translation (contracts, certificates, official filings)
  • Certified translation coordination for AEAT, notary, and registry submissions
  • Localisation of business communications, terms & conditions, and client-facing materials

07

Representation Before the AEAT

Spanish tax authority communications arrive in Spanish — dense, bureaucratic, and time-sensitive. With a signed authorisation, we take over all contact with the AEAT on your behalf.

  • Formal representation via signed power of authorisation (representación ante la AEAT)
  • Receipt and management of all AEAT notifications, requerimientos, and correspondence
  • Response drafting and submission in Spanish on your behalf
  • Clear English (or Russian) summaries of every communication received
  • Monitoring of deadlines for responses, appeals, and filings
  • Coordination with your tax advisor or legal counsel in your home country

Missing an AEAT notification or responding incorrectly can trigger penalties and investigations. Having a Spanish-speaking representative monitoring your tax mailbox removes that risk entirely.
